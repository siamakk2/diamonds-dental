Quick ReferenceReferencia Rápida

The practice, in brief.El consultorio, en breve.

Where is Diamond's Dental Inc. located?

Diamond's Dental Inc. is at 331 E La Habra Blvd, La Habra, CA 90631, with free on-site parking. The office serves patients from La Habra, La Habra Heights, Whittier, Brea, Fullerton, Buena Park, La Mirada and Hacienda Heights.

What are the office hours?

Monday through Thursday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday, 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM. The office is closed Fridays and Sundays.

What services does Diamond's Dental Inc. provide?

General and preventive dentistry including exams, cleanings, sealants and fillings; restorative treatment including crowns, bridges, root canals and extractions; orthodontics with braces for teens and adults; and cosmetic dentistry including professional teeth whitening.

Does the office speak Spanish?

Yes. Diamond's Dental Inc. is fully bilingual — se habla español — from the front desk through treatment, including insurance and cost discussions.

What insurance is accepted?

All PPO dental plans are accepted, and the office is in-network with Delta, Aetna, Liberty, Ameritas, Guardian, Cigna, GEHA, Humana, United Healthcare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, MetLife, United Concordia, UNUM and Astivia. Medicare is also accepted.

How often should I see a dentist?

Most patients are seen twice a year for an exam and cleaning. Preventive visits are typically the best-covered category on a dental plan and catch problems while they are still small. Your dentist may recommend a different interval based on your own risk factors.
