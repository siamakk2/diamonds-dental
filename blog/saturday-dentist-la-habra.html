Finding a Saturday Dentist in La Habra and North Orange County

Weekday-only dental hours quietly push preventive care out of reach for shift workers, commuters and families. Here's how Saturday appointments change that — and how to actually get one.

Diamond's Dental Inc. · 331 E La Habra Blvd, La Habra, CA · (562) 448-3018 · Updated 2026-08-18

Short answer: Diamond's Dental Inc. at 331 E La Habra Blvd is open Saturdays from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM, plus Monday through Thursday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Saturday appointments fill first, so call (562) 448-3018 ahead rather than the same morning.

Why weekday-only dental hours are a real barrier

North Orange County runs on commutes. Patients travelling from La Habra, La Habra Heights, Whittier, Brea, Fullerton, Buena Park, La Mirada and Hacienda Heights are frequently driving into Los Angeles, Anaheim or Irvine for work. A "quick cleaning" scheduled at 2:00 PM on a Tuesday is rarely quick — it's a half day of PTO, or an hour of unpaid time for anyone paid hourly.

The result is predictable and expensive: preventive visits get postponed until something hurts. Pain does not respect business hours, and the treatment that follows is almost always bigger than the cleaning that was skipped.

What Saturday hours change

A Saturday slot removes the tradeoff between keeping your teeth and keeping your paycheck. In practice, it matters most for:

  • Hourly and shift workers, for whom a weekday appointment is a direct pay cut.
  • Families with school-age children, who would otherwise pull kids out of class — and often need to bring siblings along.
  • Orthodontic patients, since braces require regular adjustment visits over many months. A weekday-only schedule turns one treatment into a year of missed afternoons.
  • Anyone commuting out of the area, where a 9-to-6 weekday window is functionally closed.

Getting a Saturday appointment that actually works

Weekend capacity is finite everywhere, so a little strategy helps:

  • Book the next one before you leave. Scheduling your following visit at checkout is the single most reliable way to get the slot you want.
  • Ask about the first appointment of the day. Early Saturday slots tend to run closest to on-time, since delays accumulate as the day goes on.
  • Stack the family. Booking siblings back-to-back turns three trips into one.
  • Call, don't wait. If something hurts on a Friday, call rather than hoping it settles over the weekend. Dental pain rarely improves on its own.
