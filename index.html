Common QuestionsPreguntas Frecuentes

Answers before you call.Respuestas antes de llamar.

Is Diamond's Dental Inc. open on Saturdays?¿Están abiertos los sábados?

Yes. We're open Saturdays from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM, and Monday through Thursday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We're closed Fridays and Sundays. Saturday hours exist so patients don't have to miss work or school.Sí. Abrimos los sábados de 8:00 AM a 3:00 PM, y de lunes a jueves de 9:00 AM a 6:00 PM. Cerramos viernes y domingos.

Do you speak Spanish?¿Hablan español?

Yes — se habla español. Our practice is fully bilingual from the front desk through treatment, and we confirm every appointment in your preferred language.Sí, se habla español. Nuestro consultorio es completamente bilingüe, desde la recepción hasta el tratamiento.

What insurance do you accept?¿Qué seguros aceptan?

We accept all PPO plans and are in-network with Delta, Aetna, Liberty, Ameritas, Guardian, Cigna, GEHA, Humana, United Healthcare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, MetLife, United Concordia, UNUM and Astivia. We also accept Medicare, bill your insurance directly, and will give you a free estimate of your coverage.Aceptamos todos los planes PPO y estamos en red con Delta, Aetna, Liberty, Ameritas, Guardian, Cigna, GEHA, Humana, United Healthcare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, MetLife, United Concordia, UNUM y Astivia. También aceptamos Medicare.

What should I bring to my first visit?¿Qué debo traer a mi primera visita?

Bring your insurance card and a photo ID — both are required at each visit. Cash and most major credit cards are accepted at the time of your visit.Traiga su tarjeta de seguro e identificación con foto — ambos se requieren en cada visita.

Do you offer braces?¿Ofrecen frenos?

Yes. We provide braces for both teenagers and adults, including Saturday adjustment visits so treatment fits around school and work schedules.Sí. Ofrecemos frenos para adolescentes y adultos, incluyendo ajustes los sábados.

Is there parking?¿Hay estacionamiento?

Yes — free parking is available on site at 331 E La Habra Blvd.Sí, estacionamiento gratuito en el sitio, en 331 E La Habra Blvd.
